Frequently asked questions

What tailoring services do you offer?

Men's shirts, trousers, suits and blazers, traditional wear, women's blouses and dresses, alterations, uniforms, children's clothing and custom occasion outfits. For anything else, ask us on WhatsApp.

How do I request an appointment?

Fill in the appointment form and send it on WhatsApp, or call either number. Your request is not confirmed until the shop replies with the date and time.

How are prices confirmed?

Prices depend on the garment, fabric and design. The shop confirms the final price with you before stitching begins.

Do you offer alterations?

Yes. We offer alterations and fitting adjustments. Contact us to discuss what you need.

How are delivery dates agreed?

We agree a delivery date with you when you place the order, based on the work involved.

How can I contact the shop?

Call either number, message us on WhatsApp, or send an enquiry using the form in the Contact section.

Privacy notice

This website does not store the details you type into its forms. When you send a form, it opens WhatsApp with a message you review and send yourself. Body measurements should be shared in person at the shop. Tapping directions opens Google Maps, which has its own privacy policy.
